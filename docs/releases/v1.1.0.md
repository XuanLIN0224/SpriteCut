## Download SpriteCut

Download **SpriteCut.zip** under **Assets**. Extract the ZIP and open **SpriteCut.html** in your browser. The editor runs offline.

## New in v1.1.0

- **No gradients** in the Colors panel turns SVG gradients into solid fills and simplifies smooth shading in PNG and JPEG artwork.
- Automatic SVG flattening favors a visible non-white color stop. You can still choose the first, middle, or last stop yourself.
- PNG export keeps flat color boundaries sharp. The 1080p and 5 px defaults, custom palettes, and outlines are included.

## 中文版

在下方 **Assets** 下载 **SpriteCut.zip**。解压后双击 **SpriteCut.html** 即可离线使用。新版在 **颜色** 面板新增了 **无渐变 · 输出纯色块**，可压平 SVG 渐变，也会尝试合并位图里的平滑明暗过渡。
