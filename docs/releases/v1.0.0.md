## Download SpriteCut

Download **SpriteCut.zip** below, extract it, and open **SpriteCut.html** in Chrome, Firefox, Safari, or Edge. The editor and example work offline. Your artwork stays on your device.

- Convert SVG, PNG, JPEG, or WebP into pixel art.
- Start with 1920 × 1080 at 5 × 5 pixels per block, or set your own canvas and grid.
- Set a maximum number of colors, use an exact custom palette, or flatten SVG gradients.
- Compare your original and pixel art side by side, then export a PNG.
